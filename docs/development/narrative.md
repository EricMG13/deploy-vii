# CP-1 evidence-risk-credit synthesis

`narrative_inputs(pack, context, financial, definition, binding)` creates
`narrative-inputs/1` by recomputing the cumulative gap register and a catalogue of
prior rows. `NarrativeReview` (`narrative-review/1`) binds that exact input hash.
`canonical_narrative` recomputes the inputs from the five external contracts,
checks the review and produces `canonical-narrative/1`. This is the internal
synthesis protocol. [Candidate projection](narrative-projection.md) now connects
the findings, coverage and referenced records to the Workbench and Markdown
preview. Full canonical handoff and live analysis remain unqualified.

The catalogue includes source/entity/event records, original observations,
statements, normalizations, definitions and multi-figure events, source and period
checks, cumulative gaps, periods/constructions, canonical values, KPI calculations
and computation graphs. Each entry has a namespaced ID, originating artifact hash,
exact row JSON/hash and all located citations. Row content is generated by Python,
not supplied again by the model. The complete input artifacts retain issuer,
periods, units, accounting basis, source hashes and limitations.

Each finding has an analytical dimension, title, evidence, risk mechanic and
credit implication. Passages contain prose, references to catalogue entries and
an issuer token resolved from the run. Evidence requires a prior-row reference;
risk and credit fields require prose; each finding binds the issuer explicitly.
Prose/title/limitation fields reject numeric characters, including Unicode numeric
characters, so numeric literals must come from referenced records. This does not
prove that prose is true: spelled-out quantities, causal reasoning, materiality
and generic commentary still require semantic assessment. There is no model-
authored replacement amount or new extraction in this protocol.

The seven dimensions are leverage, coverage, cash-flow quality, liquidity,
data quality, definition risks and key gaps. Coverage distinguishes addressed,
unassessed and not applicable. Addressed coverage names all supplied findings for
that dimension; not applicable needs evidence and a rationale. Missing coverage
remains a gap. Without any available numeric KPI, the scope is data-quality-only:
financial-dimension findings are rejected, while data-quality, definition and
key-gap analysis remains possible. Placeholder KPI rows alone cannot open the
financial-synthesis gate; known zero can.

Every prior subsequent event and multi-figure event requires a story tied to its
own evidence entry. The retained event record supplies dates and exclusion from
historical figures; multi-figure records retain the distinct accounting roles.
A story ID alone cannot clear the obligation: its findings must cite that actual
record in their evidence chain. Missing required stories remain BLOCK gaps.

Funding assessment is explicit analyst synthesis over prior located balance-sheet
rows. An unassessed scan stays a gap; reviewed scope and material row IDs are
separate. Material items require named issuer-specific detail, not a standard
asset, debt, ordinary payable or aggregate row. The declared classification still
requires semantic verification. Each declared material non-debt funding row
requires a named story; the chain must explain operating funding, potential
refund/performance outflows and credit implications using prior evidence.
Unreviewed located balance-sheet rows remain a coverage gap. A no-material-items
conclusion applies only to the explicitly reviewed scope, not unseen sources.
This does not insert a newly extracted liability into the financial book.

All upstream gaps remain in the narrative report. Coverage omissions, funding
review limits, missing required stories and review limitations append new rows;
none can waive an inherited BLOCK or change a prior number. Status remains
candidate/restricted/blocked and analytical qualification is fixed to unrun.

`validate_narrative_review` validates structure against its supplied input bundle,
including recomputed catalogue and scope. It cannot authenticate the source pack
by itself. `validate_canonical_narrative` recomputes the full report from the five
external source contracts and the externally expected narrative review, detecting
substitution or output tampering. Use that boundary for artifact acceptance checks.
Structural success alone never grants analytical or Module acceptance.
