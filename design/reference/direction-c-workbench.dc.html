CDirection C: Workbench. Icon rail, a navigator panel, a dense panelled canvas and a persistent inspector where every figure's passport lands.
C1Statement: thesis, colour, type, components, risk
Thesis
An analyst's workbench: an icon rail for destinations, a navigator that lists and filters the modules, a dense panelled canvas with a navy verdict bar, and a persistent inspector pane, so a figure's source is one click and zero popovers away.
Honest risk
Three panes at 1280px is tight: the navigator must start closed on laptops, leaving the rail and the inspector, and a PM who only wants the call may find the chrome heavier than the page. Mono figures read as an instrument, which some readers will call a spreadsheet.

Component language
The call: a 56px navy verdict bar across the canvas; each item is a key, a signal-coloured position tile and the word; a segmented meter follows. Always at the top of the Front Page; the canvas scrolls beneath it.
Panels: square-cornered white panels with an 11px caps head, owner code and a state word; panels can hold tabs (Capital structure | EBITDA adjustments).
Tables: 12px mono figures, 1px rules, zebra off, hover row; headroom bars, meters and severity bars in CSS.
Charts: SVG, same kit; the inspector shows the table twin of the focused chart.
Status and verdict: icon + word, signal colour on text, soft tint behind; on navy the pale tints.
Provenance as a glyph before the figure: cited document; calculated equals-box; synthetic flask; gap ∅ G8; illustrative: a dashed tile; model-written: a pen glyph at the panel head.
Icons: one 16px set; the rail is icon-only with tooltips and labels in the navigator; document-type icons in the inspector.

How it holds 17. The navigator lists every module in plan order at 24px a row, numbered, with a family colour bar at the left and a status icon; the filter row shows only the modules with limitations or blocks. Seventeen rows plus the header and the page outline fit in 900px. Collapsed (the default below 1280px), the icon rail keeps the six destinations with status dots, and the switcher on / reaches any module, figure or source by typing. The cost: with the navigator closed, the module list is one keystroke away rather than always visible.
